Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Last Text at One Percent"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-last-text-at-one-percent-ba9fc876) — the funniest final text a dying phone sends at one percent battery, under 200 characters.

## The entry

> Battery at 1%. My last text: I love you, the charger is across the room, and I accept my fate. Tell the plants I tried.

## How it works

Run `python3 main.py` and it writes the entry text above to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline. Deterministic, no network, no other output files.
